# Advanced Insights Patch My PC "Activity" Dashboard

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/16 (1).jpg>)

The Patch My PC Activity dashboard shows summary and detail information of all update and application activities in your environment resulting from the Patch My PC service. Information regarding your Patch My PC license and all applications and updates published in ConfigMgr is shown across the statistics and tables.

Note, the bottom right "Detected Patch My PC Updates not Synchronised table will show all updates relevant to your environment which you have not selected in the Patch My PC Publisher, this shows where ther eis an opportunity for you to improve compliance further by including new products from our catalog. This table is available only in Advanced Insights, as it relies on our Inventory Extensions which are a feature of our Premium Suite.
