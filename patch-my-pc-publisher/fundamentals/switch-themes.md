# Switch Themes in Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

Clicking the **Theme** control at the bottom of Patch My PC (PMPC) Publisher lets you switch between **Light mode** and **Dark mode** to find the one that suits you best.
