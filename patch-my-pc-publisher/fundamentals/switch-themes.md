# Switch Themes in Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

Clicking the **Theme** control dropdown in the header of Patch My PC (PMPC) Publisher lets you switch between the following themes:

* Light
* Dark
* High Contrast

Find the one that suits you best.
