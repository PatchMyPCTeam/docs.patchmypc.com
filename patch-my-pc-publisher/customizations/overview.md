# Overview of Customizations in Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

You can tailor third-party application updates and deployments using the right-click options available in Patch My PC (PMPC) Publisher. These actions let you configure scripts, command-line options, transforms, installation behavior, and many other settings to match your environment’s needs.
