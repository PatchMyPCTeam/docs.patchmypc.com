# Disable email for manual sync in Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

When the **Disable email for manual sync** option is enabled (it is disabled by default), email notifications are sent only for scheduled syncs.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1320).png" alt="&#x27;Disable email for manual sync&#x27; option" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Important**

Email notifications are not sent when a sync is initiated manually from the [Sync](../../sync-tab/) tab.
{% endhint %}
