# Overview of ConfigMgr Apps in Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Important**</p>
<p>This article has not been updated for Version 3.x. Once it has, this banner will be removed.</p>
</blockquote>

The **ConfigMgr Apps** tab in Patch My PC (PMPC) Publisher is where you select which third-party applications should be published to ConfigMgr. Products enabled here determine which third-party applications the Publisher will publish and maintain within your environment.

![ConfigMgr Apps Tab Overview](/_images/image-(489).png "ConfigMgr Apps Tab Overview")

Third-party applications appear in the **Software Library > Application Management > Applications** node in the ConfigMgr console.

![Applications appear in the Application Management view](/_images/image-(490).png "Applications appear in the Application Management view")

Additional behavior related to application publishing can be configured using the [**Options**](../../../patch-my-pc-publisherv2/administration/configmgr-apps/options/) button on the ConfigMgr Apps tab.
