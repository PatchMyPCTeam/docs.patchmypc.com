# Upgrade Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

When a new production release of Patch My PC (PMPC) Publisher is available, your instance of Publisher will be automatically upgraded.

Once the upgrade completes, you will see the **Welcome banner** that details the version you are now running. Clicking **See Overview of Changes** takes you to the documentation where you can learn more about what has changed in this new release.
