# Help and Resources section of Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

The **HELP & RESOURCES** section on the **About** tab of Patch My PC (PMPC) Publisher provides links to various useful Publisher-related resources.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1308).png" alt="&#x27;HELP &#x26; RESOURCES&#x27; section" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The links in this section are:

* [Release notes](../../release-notes/production-releases.md) - Links to the Release notes page for Publisher.
* [Contact us](https://patchmypc.com/contact-us/) - Opens the contact us page on our website, in case you need to contact us.
* [Technical support](https://patchmypc.com/technical-support/) - Allows you to open a Support Case
* [Support forum](https://forum.patchmypc.com/index.php/board,23.0.html) - Links to our support forum where you can search for common issues or start a new thread.
* [Request an app](https://ideas.patchmypc.com/) - Opens our Ideas and Feedback site, where you can submit ideas and feedback for our products.
* [License agreement](https://patchmypc.com/legal/terms-of-service/) - Opens our **Terms of Service**, which you agree to by using Publisher.
