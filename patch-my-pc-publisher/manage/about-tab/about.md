# About section of Patch My PC Publisher

_Applies to: Patch My PC Publisher V3.x_

The **ABOUT** section on the **About** tab of Patch My PC (PMPC) Publisher shows key version information for Publisher, allows you to restart the publishing service, and upgrade to a later version.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1302).png" alt="&#x27;ABOUT&#x27; section" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This section consists of the following fields:

## **Currently Installed Version**

Shows the version of Publisher currently installed.

## Status field

Shows the current service state of the Publishing Service, which should be **Running** during normal operation.&#x20;

## **Latest Version**

Shows the latest available version of Publisher.

## **Last Check for New Version**

Shows the last date and time that Publisher checked for a new version.

## Restart Service button

Clicking the **Restart Service** button restarts the Publishing Service and refreshes the current service state. This is useful after configuration changes or troubleshooting scenarios.

## Upgrade Now button

If a newer version of Publisher is available and assigned to your update ring, the **Upgrade Now** button is available. Patch My PC uses a ringed deployment model for Publisher, which means updates may be available on demand depending on your assigned ring.
