# Onboard to Intune Apps for Custom Apps users

_Applies to: Intune Apps for Cloud_

If you are already a Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud Custom Apps user and also intend to deploy apps from our App Catalog, you must establish a connection to your Intune Tenant using the [Add an Intune Connection](../../manage/settings/connections/add-connection.md#add-an-intune-connection) process.
