# Modify a Custom App

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud Custom Apps_

You may need to make some changes to a Patch My PC (PMPC) Custom App such as changing it’s configuration or adding additional installation files.

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Note**</p>
<p>See [Updating a Custom App](update-a-custom-app.md) for details on how to update an existing Custom App to a later version.</p>
</blockquote>

To modify a Custom App:

1. Sign in to the PMPC portal [https://portal.patchmypc.com/](https://portal.patchmypc.com/).
2.  On the **App Catalog** page, search for the relevant app.<br>

    ![Searching for your Custom App](/_images/image-(2717).png "Searching for your Custom App")
3. Click the app to open it.
4.  On the app’s properties page, click **Edit Info**.<br>

    ![Clicking 'Edit Info'](/_images/image-(2718).png "Clicking &#x27;Edit Info&#x27;")

The Custom Apps **Edit Application** Wizard starts.

![Custom Apps 'Edit Application' Wizard](/_images/image-(4501).png "Custom Apps &#x27;Edit Application&#x27; Wizard")

5. Make the required changes on the relevant tabs, then click **Save**.

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Note**</p>
<p>You cannot modify the **Installer Type** of a Custom App.</p>
</blockquote>

![Clicking 'Save'](/_images/image-(3336).png "Clicking &#x27;Save&#x27;")

The App Catalog is displayed along with the following notification:\
\
**Success – Your application has been successfully edited**

!['Success – Your application has been successfully edited' notification](/_images/image-(2721).png "&#x27;Success – Your application has been successfully edited&#x27; notification")
