# Create a Microsoft Teams Webhook Notification in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

To create a Microsoft Teams (Teams) webhook notification in Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud:

1. Navigate to **Settings | Notifications**
2. Create the webhook if you haven’t already, as detailed in the [Creating a Microsoft Teams Webhook](technical-references/webhooks-reference/create-teams-webhook.md) process.
3. Follow the [Create a Webhook notification in Cloud](create-webhook-notification.md) and select **Microsoft Teams** from the **Add Webhook** dropdown.
4.  On the **Add Microsoft Teams** screen, enter a unique name for the webhook in the **Webhook Name** field. For example, **Patch My PC**.<br>

    ![](/_images/image-(2303).png)
5. In the **Webhook URL** field, enter the unique URL of your Teams webhook created in Step 1. and click **Save**.

![](/_images/image-(2304).png)

The new webhook notification is shown under the **Webhook Notifications** section.

![New webhook notification](/_images/image-(294).png "New webhook notification")

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Tip**</p>
<p>You can click the small envelope beside the webhook URL (![](/_images/image-(2602).png>)) to send a test notification. See [Testing a Microsoft Teams Webhook Notification](technical-references/test-teams-webhook-notification.md) for more details.</p>
</blockquote>

6. Click **Save** to save the newly added notification.

![Clicking 'Save'](/_images/image-(295).png "Clicking &#x27;Save&#x27;")

The **Success – Notification created** notification is shown.

![Success notification](/_images/image-(296).png "Success notification")

The Cloud Portal auto-refreshes to show the new notification.

![Cloud portal auto-refreshing to show the new notification](/_images/image-(297).png "Cloud portal auto-refreshing to show the new notification")
