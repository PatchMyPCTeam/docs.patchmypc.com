# Test an Email Notification in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

You can test an email notification from Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud both at the time it is created and afterwards.

## Testing an email notification at the time of creation

1. Follow the [Create an Email Notification](../create-email-notification.md) process.
2. Once you have entered and saved the relevant email addresses, click the small envelope beside an email address () to send a test message to that email address.

![Clicking the small envelope beside any of the email addresses to send them a test message](../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-\(287\).png)

The Cloud Portal will send a test email and display the **Success - Test email has been sent** notification.

!['Success - Test email has been sent' notification](../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-\(288\).png)

If the email address is valid, the user will receive an email with the following details:\
\
**Sender:** Patch My PC\<noreply@patchmypc.com>\
**Subject:** Test notification from Patch My PC Cloud

![Test email showing email notifications are working](../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-\(2595\).png)

> \*\*Tip\*\*
>
> If the user does not receive the email, check the email is not in their junk email folder.

## Testing an email notification after it has been created

1. Follow the [Modify a Notification](../modify-notification.md) process and select the relevant Slack notification you want to test.
2. Click the small envelope beside an email address () to send a test message to that email address.

![Clicking the small envelope beside any of the email addresses to send them a test message](../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-\(289\).png)

The Cloud Portal will send a test email and display the **Success - Test email has been sent** notification.

!['Success - Test email has been sent' notification](../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-\(290\).png)

If the email address is valid, the user will receive an email with the following details:\

**Subject:** Test notification from Patch My PC Cloud

![Test email showing email notifications are working](../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-\(2595\).png)
