# Modify a Notification in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

To modify a notification in Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud:

1. Navigate to **Settings | Notifications**
2. On the **Notifications** page, click the pencil icon beside the notification you want to modify.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3843).png" alt="Clicking the pencil icon" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Make the required changes, then click **Save** to save your changes.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3844).png" alt="Clicking &#x27;Save&#x27;" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Success - Notification updated** notification is shown.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3845).png" alt="&#x27;Success&#x27; notification" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Cloud Portal auto-refreshes to show the modified notification.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3846).png" alt="Modified notification" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
