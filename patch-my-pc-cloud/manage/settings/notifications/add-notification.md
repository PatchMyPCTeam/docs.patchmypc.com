# Add a Notification in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

To add a notification in Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud:

1. Navigate to **Settings | Notifications**
2. On the **Notifications** page, click **Add Notification**.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (291).png" alt="Clicking &#x27;Add Notification&#x27;" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. On the **Add Notifications** screen, enter a unique name for this notification in the **Name** field.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (292).png" alt="Entering a unique name" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Follow the relevant process for the type of notification you are creating:

* [Create a Webhook notification](create-webhook-notification.md)
* [Create an Email notification](create-email-notification.md)
