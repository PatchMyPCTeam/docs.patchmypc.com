# Enable Company Recovery in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

The _Enable Company Recovery_ feature in Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud provides a way for you to recover access to a PMPC Cloud Company should you lose access.

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Note**</p>
<p>To prevent access issues to your Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud company, we highly recommend granting at least two users the [Full Admin with Access Management](../users/user-roles-reference.md) user role.</p>
<p>If you lose access to your company, you can try following the [Recover your Company](recover-company.md) process, but this is dependent on Company Recovery being enabled, as detailed below.</p>
</blockquote>

To enable Company Recovery:

1. Navigate to **Settings | Company**
2. Scroll down to the **Company Recovery** section.

![Scrolling to the 'Company Recovery' section](/_images/image-(4376).png "Scrolling to the &#x27;Company Recovery&#x27; section")

3. Click the **Enable Company Recovery** setting to enable it, then click **Save**

![Clicking to 'Enable Company Recovery' then clicking 'Save'](/_images/image-(4377).png "Clicking to &#x27;Enable Company Recovery&#x27; then clicking &#x27;Save&#x27;")

The **Success - Company information updated** notification is shown.
