# Enable a Domain in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud_

To enable an Entra ID Custom Domain for your Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud company:

1. Sign in to your PMPC Cloud company using a user account that has the **Full Admin with Access Management** role.
2. Navigate to **Settings | Company**

![Navigating to ‘Settings | Company'](<../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-(559) (1).png>)

3. Click the **Domains** tab.

![Clicking the ‘Domains' tab](<../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-(560) (1).png>)

4. Select the checkbox beside the relevant domain you want to enable, then click **Save**

> \*\*Note\*\*
>
> By default, all custom domains are enabled when created in Entra ID.

![Selecting the checkbox beside the relevant domain you want to enable and clicking ‘Save'](<../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-(561) (1).png>)

The **Success – Domains updated** notification is shown.

![‘Success – Domains updated' notification](<../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image-(562) (1).png>)

Once a domain has been enabled, any users within that domain can click **Request Access** on the **Sign In** page to request access to this company as detailed in [Onboard to Cloud](../../../../onboard-cloud.md) .
