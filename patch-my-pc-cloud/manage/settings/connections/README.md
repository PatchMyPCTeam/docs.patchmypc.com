# Manage Connections in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud and Publisher_

Once onboarded to Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud, you can manage all of your connections to our Publisher and Intune from the **Connections** node of the Cloud Portal by navigating to **Settings | Connections**

![Navigating to 'Settings | Connections'](/_images/image-(340 "Navigating to 'Settings | Connections'") (1).png>)

The **Connections** page is then displayed, showing any existing connections to Intune or from Publisher and allowing you to:

* [Add a new Connection](add-connection.md)
* [Reconnect a Connection](reconnect-connection.md)
* [Disconnect a Connection](disconnect-connection.md)
* [View Connections](view-connections.md)
