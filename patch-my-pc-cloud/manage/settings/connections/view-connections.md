# View Connections in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud and Publisher_

To view all of the current connections to your Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud Portal:

1. Sign in to the Portal at [https://portal.patchmypc.com/](https://portal.patchmypc.com/).
2. Navigate to **Settings | Connections**

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (340).png" alt="Navigating to &#x27;Settings | Connections&#x27;" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Connections** page shows any current connections and their status.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (313).png" alt="&#x27;Connections&#x27; page showing any current connections and their status" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note**

If your Intune connection has a yellow triangle in the **Status** column, if means a permissions issue has been detected. See [Why do I see a Yellow triangle beside my Intune Connection in Patch My PC Cloud?](../../../troubleshoot/intune-apps/why-do-i-see-a-yellow-triangle-beside-my-intune-connection-in-patch-my-pc-cloud.md) for more details.
{% endhint %}
