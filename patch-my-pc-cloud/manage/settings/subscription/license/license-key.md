# Manage your License Key in Patch My PC Cloud

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

You can perform the following management-related tasks for your Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud License Key:

* [View your License Key](license-key.md#view-your-license-key)
* [Copy your License Key](license-key.md#copy-your-license-key)
* [Activate a new License](license-key.md#activate-a-new-license)
* [Use a Different License Key](license-key.md#use-a-different-license-key)

To perform any of these tasks:

1. Sign in to the Cloud Portal.
2. Navigate to **Settings | Subscription**

![Navigating to 'Settings | Subscription'](/_images/image-(4217).png "Navigating to &#x27;Settings | Subscription&#x27;")

## View your License Key

To view your current PMPC Cloud license key, click the _eye_ icon:

![Clicking the eye icon](/_images/image-(4218).png "Clicking the eye icon")

Your full license key will then be shown:

![Full license key shown](/_images/image-(4219).png "Full license key shown")

## Copy your License Key

To copy your current PMPC Cloud license key, click the license key.

![Clicking your license key](/_images/image-(4221).png "Clicking your license key")

The **Success - License Key copied** notification is shown.

![The 'Success - License Key copied' notification is shown](/_images/image-(4220).png "The &#x27;Success - License Key copied&#x27; notification is shown")

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Note**</p>
<p>Only user accouts granted the following roles can copy license keys:</p>
<p>* Full Admin With Access Management</p>
<p>* Full Admin</p>
<p>* Intune App Admin</p>
</blockquote>

## Activate a new License

To activate a license key for a new company:

1. Click the pencil icon beside the **License Key** field.

![Clicking the pencil icon](/_images/image-(4224).png "Clicking the pencil icon")

2. Paste/type the license key into the **Enter License Key** field, then click **Activate Now**.

![Entering the license key into the 'Enter License Key field' and clicking 'Activate Now'](/_images/image-(4230).png "Entering the license key into the &#x27;Enter License Key field&#x27; and clicking &#x27;Activate Now&#x27;")

The **You have successfully activated your license** screen is displayed, which you can click **Close** on to dismiss.

!['You have successfully activated your license' screen](/_images/image-(4225).png "&#x27;You have successfully activated your license&#x27; screen")



The **License Key** field updates to show the newly activated license.

![Updated 'License Key' field](/_images/image-(4226).png "Updated &#x27;License Key&#x27; field")

## Use a Different License Key

To enter a different license key:

1. Click the pencil icon beside the **License Key** field.

![Clicking the pencil icon](/_images/image-(4224).png "Clicking the pencil icon")

The **License Screen** is shown, providing details of your current license.

!['License Screen' showing providing details of your current license](/_images/image-(3573).png "&#x27;License Screen&#x27; showing providing details of your current license")

To enter a new license key, paste/type it into the **Enter License Key** field, then click **Activate Now**

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Note**</p>
<p>If you have already activated a different license key, it will show under the **Use Existing License** section. To switch to a different key, click the relevant license followed by **Select** which will switch your Cloud Company to use this License Key.</p>
</blockquote>

![Entering the license key and clicking 'Activate Now'](/_images/image-(4227).png "Entering the license key and clicking &#x27;Activate Now&#x27;")

The **You have successfully activated your license** screen is displayed, which you can click **Close** on to dismiss.

!['You have successfully activated your license' screen](/_images/image-(4225).png "&#x27;You have successfully activated your license&#x27; screen")

The **License Key** field updates to show the newly activated license.

![Updated 'License Key' field](/_images/image-(4226).png "Updated &#x27;License Key&#x27; field")
