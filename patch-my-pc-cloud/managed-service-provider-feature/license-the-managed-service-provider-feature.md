# License the Managed Service Provider Feature

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

Once the parent MSP company has been onboarded to Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud, you must configure it to use an MSP Plus license.

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Important**</p>
<p>Once you license a company with an MSP license, you cannot switch that company to a different PMPC Cloud license type.</p>
</blockquote>

To configure a company to use an MSP Plus license:

1. Follow the [Manage your License](/broken/pages/8qjfvVWkH0JTJRXsuS6m) process and enter the MSP Plus license key on the **Enter License Key** field, then click **Activate Now**.
2. Click **Close** on the **You have successfully activated your license** dialog box.\
   \
   ![	Clicking "Close" on the "You have successfully activated your license" dialog box](/_images/image-(1077 "	Clicking \"Close\" on the \"You have successfully activated your license\" dialog box").png>)<br>
3. As the terms and conditions for the MSP Plus license differ from the other PMPC Cloud license types, click **Terms and Conditions**.\
   \
   ![Clicking "Terms and Conditions"](/_images/image-(1078 "Clicking \"Terms and Conditions\"").png>)<br>
4.  Review the Terms and Conditions and once you are happy, click the **Accept all Terms and Conditions** checkbox followed by **Confirm**.\
    \
    ![Click the "Accept all Terms and conditions" checkbox followed by "Confirm"](/_images/image-(1079 "Click the \"Accept all Terms and conditions\" checkbox followed by \"Confirm\"").png>)\
    \
    Once you have clicked **Confirm**, the portal refreshes and the **MSP Customers** node appears at the top of the portal.<br>

    ![Portal refreshes and the "MSP Customers" node appears at the top of the portal](/_images/image-(1080).png "Portal refreshes and the “MSP Customers” node appears at the top of the portal")

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>**Note**</p>
<p>MSP Companies (both parent and child) are subject to the limits detailed in [Patch My PC Cloud Product limits](../product-limits.md).</p>
<p>Also, if an MSP license expires, then the MSP tenant and all customers linked to it will be switched to read-only mode.</p>
</blockquote>
