# View all MSP Custom Apps

_Applies to: Patch My PC Cloud_

To locate any Custom Apps for Managed Service Providers (MSPs) you’ve created in Patch My PC (PMPC) Cloud:

1.  In the toolbar, click the filter button.<br>

    ![Clicking the filter button](/_images/image-(2965).png)
2.  Under the **Source** section, select the checkbox for the relevant company that created the Custom App and click **Apply All Filters**.<br>

    ![Selecting the checkbox for the relevant company that created the Custom App and clicking "Apply All Filters](/_images/image-(2966).png)

    \
    \
    The list of apps in the App Catalog is filtered to show only the relevant Custom Apps for MSPs created on the selected company.<br>

    ![Filtered App Catalog list](/_images/image-(2967).png)

> \*\*Tip\*\*
>
> Don’t forget to click \*\*Clear All\*\* when you’ve finished working with the filters.
