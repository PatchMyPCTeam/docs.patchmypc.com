# Delete the Patch My PC Client Manually

_Applies to: Patch My PC Client_

To manually delete a Patch My PC (PMPC) Client:

1. Navigate to **Advanced/Patch Insights | Home**

![Navigating to ‘Advanced/Patch Insights | Home'](/_images/image-(3801).png "Navigating to ‘Advanced/Patch Insights | Home’")

2. Scroll down to the Charts section and in the top right-hand corner of the **Client Information** chart, click the ellipsis followed by **View Chart Data**

![Clicking the ellipsis followed by 'View Chart Data'](/_images/image-(4547).png "Clicking the ellipsis followed by &#x27;View Chart Data&#x27;")

The **Client Version** device list modal is displayed.

!['Client Version' device list modal](/_images/image-(4548).png "&#x27;Client Version&#x27; device list modal")

3. Check the checkbox beside the client(s) you want to delete, then from the **Action** menu, select **Delete x Device**&#x20;

![Selecting the client to be deleted and deleting it.](/_images/image-(4549).png "Selecting the client to be deleted and deleting it.")

4. On the **Are you sure** dialog, click **OK** to confirm the deletion.

![Clicking 'OK' on the 'Are you sure' dialog](/_images/image-(4550).png "Clicking &#x27;OK&#x27; on the &#x27;Are you sure&#x27; dialog")

The **Client Version** device list modal is redisplayed, showing the client has been deleted along with the **Success - 1 devices deleted** notification.

!['Client Version' device list modal showing the client has been deleted along with the success notification](/_images/image-(4551).png "&#x27;Client Version&#x27; device list modal showing the client has been deleted along with the success notification")
